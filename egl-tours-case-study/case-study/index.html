From travel inspiration to connected customer journeys.

THE CHALLENGE

Travel decisions are built over more than one touchpoint.

For a travel brand, a customer journey can begin with a social post or an advert, continue at an in-person seminar and end with a booking conversation. EGL Tours needed those moments to work together—not as isolated systems.

The existing journey made it harder to connect online interest, event participation, transaction history and the context that frontline teams need to serve customers well. The objective was a practical membership ecosystem that makes participation simpler while building a clearer customer picture over time.

Disconnected O2O journeySiloed customer dataManual event workflowsLimited frontline context

THREE BARRIERS TO MOMENTUM

Where the travel journey lost continuity.

01

Interest did not always become attendance

Campaigns could drive discovery, but registration and check-in needed a simpler, traceable bridge into offline seminars.

02

Context was scattered across systems

Bookings, offers, event participation and digital interactions lived apart, limiting usable member insight.

03

Teams needed more agility

With frequent new travel promotions and events, marketers needed to configure journeys without a heavy manual process.

A single membership experience gives customers a clearer route to rewards, activities and travel offers.

THE SOLUTION

A membership platform designed around the complete journey.

Motherapp brought core membership and event interactions into a connected experience: an app and Web App for customers, operational tools for teams, and data flows that help marketing and frontline staff act with more context.

  • Tiered rewards and member benefits to encourage ongoing travel engagement.
  • One-click registration and QR check-in for seminars and events.
  • Connected member records spanning transactions, activity and interactions.
  • Multi-channel outreach across SMS, WhatsApp, email and app push.
