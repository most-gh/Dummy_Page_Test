CASE STUDY / LIFESTYLE RETAIL / HONG KONG

One membership experience, wherever customers shop.

Club KAPOK partnered with Motherapp to connect Shopify, in-store POS and CRM data into a loyalty experience that follows members across digital and physical retail.

THE BRIEFGive every member one recognisable identity, one view of rewards and one route to engage—online, in store and at events.

THE CHALLENGE

Retail loyalty breaks down when member identity does.

Club KAPOK has a vibrant community across online retail, physical stores and frequent member events. But its Shopify and POS memberships had developed separately, creating different records, different reward experiences and an incomplete picture of customer behaviour.

The brief was not simply to launch an app. It was to create one practical operating layer where rewards, tiers, purchases, event participation and communications could work together.

Separate online & store dataManual event workflowMigration riskLimited campaign visibility

THE MOMENTS THAT NEEDED CONNECTING

Three barriers to a consistent retail relationship.

01

Different channels, different recognition

Without a shared member profile, a customer's status and rewards could feel different online and in store.

02

Events were difficult to connect

Registration and check-in relied on manual tools, leaving participation outside the member journey.

03

Insight arrived too late

Disconnected data made it harder to understand what events, offers and customer actions were actually working together.

Rewards, membership interaction and event engagement brought into one branded experience.

THE SOLUTION

A loyalty platform that connects the retail ecosystem.

Motherapp created a customised loyalty app and CRM platform around a single member identity. It brings together core retail integrations and the actions members take most often, while giving the team a more useful view of every relationship.

  • Real-time membership sync for purchases, points, rewards and tiers across Shopify and POS.
  • QR-code member cards for a simple in-store identification and reward flow.
  • Digital event journeys from in-app registration to attendance record.
  • CRM-led segmentation and automated engagement based on a connected profile.
