THE CHALLENGE

Sustainable habits needed a more connected, rewarding experience.

Carbon Wallet set out to encourage people to make lower-carbon choices in their everyday lives. The ambition was not simply to build another rewards app. It was to create a green lifestyle platform that could connect different behaviours, help people understand their impact and give them a meaningful reason to return.

The programme needed to make a range of actions easier to track and verify, while keeping the experience simple enough for regular use.

THE CHALLENGE, AT EVERYDAY SCALE

Three barriers to sustained green engagement.

01

Green actions were spread across everyday life.

Walking, public transport, recycling and sustainable shopping each happen in different contexts. Without one connected experience, users face fragmented services and extra manual effort.

02

Sustainable behaviour can be difficult to maintain.

Long-term behaviour change needs clear progress, timely feedback and rewards that make each action feel worthwhile.

03

Every action needed a different way to verify it.

Transit, step data, recycling activity and partner offers have different data flows and validation requirements. The experience still had to feel simple.

Carbon Wallet connects selected green actions with points, rewards and programme engagement.

THE SOLUTION

Carbon Wallet: a green rewards platform for everyday action.

Motherapp helped build Carbon Wallet as a connected digital experience for green missions, reward points, partner offers and carbon-saving activity. The platform turns selected sustainable behaviours into point-earning missions, helping members record actions, see progress and redeem eligible rewards through one experience.

  • Member registration and loyalty account management
  • Points, missions and reward-redemption mechanics
  • Green-action tracking and carbon-saving records
  • Partner offers and e-coupons
  • Push notifications and customer-engagement tools
  • CRM, segmentation and programme reporting

RESULTS AND IMPACT

A platform built for green engagement and a growing ecosystem.

Motherapp’s project case study reports a launch in about three months and early engagement of 25,000 monthly active users. MTR later reported a larger platform impact as Carbon Wallet continued to grow its community, partner relationships and carbon-saving activity.
